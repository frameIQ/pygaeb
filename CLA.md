## How to Sign

The Maintainer records contributor agreement through the Project's CLA assistant on
GitHub. When You open Your first pull request, an automated check will ask You to
sign by commenting:

> I have read the pyGAEB Contributor License Agreement and I hereby accept its terms.

Your GitHub username, the signing timestamp, and the commit are recorded as Your
signature. This recorded acceptance constitutes Your agreement to the terms above
and is treated as a legally binding electronic signature.

For Corporate CLAs, an authorized representative must submit the completed
*Schedule A* by opening a pull request that adds the company's file to
`.github/cla-corporate/` (one file per company, listing the designated employees).
The merged pull request is the record of corporate acceptance, and designated
employees' Contributions are accepted once it is merged.
